FLIGHT SCHOOL

One button.
All the skill.

  1. Find your wingmate. Create a room and share its invitation link, or enter their six-character code.
  2. Ready, set, flap. Everyone readies up, then the host clicks Start race. At least two players are required; empty seats do not block starting. After the countdown, press Space, click, or tap to fly upward. Gravity does the rest.
  3. Read the openings. Stay inside the luminous gates. The same seeded course awaits every player. Gaps narrow and moving gates appear as you advance. Arrows show movement direction. Watch the motion rails and anticipate faster late sweeps. V3 defaults to Hard difficulty.
  4. Keep going. A crash costs 0.75 seconds and returns you to your checkpoint. A brief shield helps you recover.
  5. First across wins and ends the round for everyone. Other pilots are ranked by progress. Cross the striped finish gate to claim the race. Then run it back.
